About me

I turn messy data into decisions people trust.

I'm a data analyst who enjoys the full journey of data — cleaning messy datasets, modeling them, and designing dashboards that make insights impossible to miss. My work spans healthcare claims, HR operations, sales analytics and applied machine learning.

From interactive Power BI reports with DAX to machine learning models in Python, I focus on one thing: making data easy to understand and act on.

How I help

Problems I've helped solve

Every project starts with a business problem — not a piece of software.

"We have data, but no idea what it's telling us."

Sales, claims or HR numbers pile up without clear answers. I turn raw data into clear stories about what's actually happening.

→ Clearer business decisions

"This report takes half my week to build."

Rebuilding the same spreadsheet by hand every week wastes hours. I build workflows and dashboards that update themselves.

→ Hours of manual work back

"We can't see our numbers until it's too late."

Waiting until month-end to spot a problem is expensive. I put the metrics that matter in one place, ready before the meeting.

→ Faster decision making

"Our data lives in five different places."

Spreadsheets, databases and exports that don't match add up to numbers nobody trusts. I clean and combine them into one source of truth.

→ Data you can actually trust
